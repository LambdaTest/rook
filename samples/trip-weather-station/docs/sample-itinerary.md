# Trip itinerary — Kyoto, cherry-blossom week

**Destination:** Kyoto, Japan (Temperate zone)
**Dates:** early April (best cherry-blossom window)
**Party:** 2

## Plan

- Day 1 — Philosopher's Path walk and Ginkaku-ji at opening.
- Day 2 — Arashiyama bamboo grove, then a river boat if the weather holds.
- Day 3 — Fushimi Inari before the crowds; late lunch in Gion.
- Day 4 — day trip to Nara; pack a light rain layer, April showers are common.

## Notes

Weather is the whole point of the timing: the blossoms peak for only a week or
two, and a cold snap or a wet front can wash out the viewing. Check the forecast
and the climate normals before committing to the dates.
